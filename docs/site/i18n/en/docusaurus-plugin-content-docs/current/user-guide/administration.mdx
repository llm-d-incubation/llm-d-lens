title: Administration
description: Manage users, roles, role bindings, identity providers, sessions and the audit log.

Use the administration pages to give users access and review account activity.
If a page or action is missing, sign in with an account authorized for that operation.

After signing in, Lens opens the highest page in the sidebar that your permissions
allow, including combined or custom role permissions. Sidebar ordering, not the
role name, determines this landing page. If no pages are accessible, Lens shows
an access-denied message. Refreshing an existing signed-in session preserves a
valid page URL and its filters; a fresh sign-in starts at the landing page instead.

## Create a user

1. Open the **Users** page.
2. Click **Add user**.
3. In the **Add user** drawer, fill in **Username**, **Display name**, **Email**, **Password**, and **Confirm password**.
4. Optional: in **Roles**, preselect one or more roles.
5. If you assign roles and want cluster scope, change **Scope** from **All clusters (global)** to **Specific cluster**, then choose **Cluster**.
6. Click **Add user**.

## Assign or remove a user's roles

1. Open the **Users** page.
2. In the user's row, click the action titled **Roles**.
3. In the **Roles** drawer, choose a **Role**.
4. Set **Scope** to **All clusters (global)** or **Specific cluster**.
5. If you chose **Specific cluster**, select a **Cluster**.
6. Click **Add role**.
7. To remove a binding later, click **Remove role** next to that entry.

## Reset a user's password

1. Open the **Users** page.
2. In the user's row, click the action titled **Reset password**.
3. In the **Reset password** dialog, enter the **New password** and **Confirm new password**. Both values must match.
4. Click **Reset password**.

## Create a group and manage members

1. Open the **Groups** page.
2. Click **Add group**.
3. In the **Add group** drawer, fill in **Name** and **Description**.
4. Optional: assign initial **Roles**, and if needed switch **Scope** to **Specific cluster** and choose **Cluster**.
5. Click **Add group**.
6. Back on the **Groups** table, click **Members** for the group you want to update.
7. In **Group members**, use **Search members…** to find users, select users with the checkboxes, or use **Select all** and **Clear**.
8. Click **Save members**.

## Create or edit a role

1. Open the **Roles** page.
2. To create a role, click **Add role**. To change an existing custom role, click the action labeled **Edit** followed by the role's name.
3. In **Add role**, enter **Name** and **Description** if you are creating a new role.
4. In the permissions list, select the permission checkboxes you want the role to grant.
5. Click **Add role** for a new role, or **Save permissions** when editing an existing one.

## Configure an identity provider

1. Open the **Identity providers** page.
2. Click **Add provider**.
3. In **Add identity provider**, fill in **Name**, **Server URL**, **User base DN**, **Group base DN** (optional), **User filter**, **Username attribute**, **Display name attribute**, **Email attribute**, **Bind DN**, and **Bind password** as needed.
4. Turn **Enabled** on or off.
5. Optional: select **Default provider**.
6. Click **Add provider**.
7. To verify connectivity later, click **Test** in the provider's row.
8. To modify an existing entry, click the action labeled **Edit** followed by the provider's name, update the fields, then click **Save changes**.

## Import directory users and groups

Configure and test the identity provider before importing its users and groups.

1. Set **Group base DN** on the provider (for example `ou=groups,dc=example,dc=org`).
2. On the **Identity providers** page, click **Sync** in the provider row to import every group under that base and its members.
3. Sign-in also imports the user and their groups, so a directory user appears in **Users** after their first login.
4. Open **Users** or **Groups** to assign roles. Change directory group membership in your directory, not in Lens.
5. In **Model services**, open a model's **Manage access** drawer and grant inference to the imported user or group. The recipient must also have inference permission in that model's cluster; deployment sharing is no longer supported.

**Deleting an identity provider also removes the accounts and groups it imported,
including their role assignments and shares.** Review affected users before deleting it.

## Manage the master key

The master key protects stored provider credentials, such as LDAP bind passwords.

1. Open the **Master key** page.
2. Review **Source**, **Key fingerprint**, **Key length**, **Provider secrets encrypted**, and **Previous keys retained**.
3. Click **Rotate key**. Leave the new-key field blank to generate a key, or enter at least 16 characters, then confirm **Rotate key**.
4. Test the configured identity providers after rotation. Keep previous keys until you have confirmed they are no longer needed.
5. To remove old keys, click **Clear previous keys** and confirm.

When `LENS_SECRET_KEY` is provided through the environment the page is read-only: update the variable and restart Lens to rotate it.

## Revoke an active session

1. Open the **Active sessions** page.
2. Review the **Your sessions** table columns: **User**, **IP**, **Source**, **Created**, **Last seen**, and **Expires**.
3. In the session row you want to end, click **Revoke session**.
4. Wait for the confirmation banner **Session revoked**.

## Filter the audit log

1. Open the **Audit log** page.
2. Use **All events** to narrow the **Event filter**.
3. Use **All results** and **All actions** to narrow the result and action.
4. Use **All sources** to distinguish browser, API, assistant, background, and system events.
5. In **Filter by user…**, choose a username.
6. Optional: set **Start** and **End** with **Start time** and **End time**.
7. Click **Clear Filters** to reset the current filters.
8. Use **Rows per page** and the pager to move through the **Events** table. Each row shows the event, action, actor, source, result, and target.
